Glossary

Sanskrit words you will hear in class and read in the teachings, with short meanings.

A B C D E G H I J K M N P R S T V Y

A

Ahamkara
Pride, ego.
Ahimsa
Non-violence.
Alasya
Laziness.
Anandamaya kosha
The sheath of bliss, the innermost of the five sheaths.
Antaranga
The inner quest: pranayama and pratyahara.
Antaratma
The innermost quest, the quest of the soul: dharana, dhyana and samadhi.
Aparigraha
Freedom from possessiveness.
Asana
Posture.
Asteya
Non-stealing.

B

Bahiranga
The outer, or external, quest: yama, niyama and asana.
Bhakti marga
The path of devotion.
Brahmacharya
Restraint, often understood as celibacy.
Buddhi
Intelligence.

C

Chitta
Consciousness, which includes the mind, the intellect and the ego.
Chitta vritti
The movements, or fluctuations, of consciousness.

D

Dharana
Concentration: attention held on one point.
Dhyana
Meditation: attention flowing without a break.
Dukha
Misery, pain.

E

Ekagra
Single-pointed concentration.

G

Ghatavastha
The intermediate stage of practice.
Guru
One who helps us gain knowledge.
Guru-shishya parampara
The tradition in which a teacher passes knowledge on to a student.

H

Hatha yoga
Self-realisation through discipline.

I

Ishvara pranidhana
Surrender to God.

J

Jnana marga
The path of knowledge.

K

Kaivalya
Emancipation, eternal freedom.
Karma marga
The path of selfless service, without thought of reward.
Klesha
Trouble, affliction.
Kosha
A sheath: one of the five layers of a person.

M

Manas
The mind.
Manomaya kosha
The mental or emotional sheath.

N

Nadi
A channel through which energy flows in the subtle body.
Nirodha
Restraint, stilling.
Nispattiavastha
The state in which body, mind and self become one.
Niyama
Disciplined personal conduct.

P

Parichayavastha
The third stage of practice, when intelligence and body become one.
Pramada
Carelessness.
Prana
Vital energy, the life force.
Pranamaya kosha
The sheath of vital energy.
Pranayama
The regulation of breath.
Pratyahara
Drawing the senses of perception inward.

R

Rajasic
The vibrant quality of nature.

S

Sadhana
Disciplined practice.
Samadhi
Absorption, self-realisation: the last of the eight limbs.
Samshaya
Doubt.
Samyama
Integration.
Santosha
Contentment.
Sarvanga sadhana
Holistic practice that brings body, mind and self together.
Sattvic
The luminous quality of nature.
Satya
Truth.
Saucha
Cleanliness, purity.
Svadhyaya
Self-study.

T

Tamasic
The inert quality of nature.
Tapas
Practice with discipline, devotion and effort.

V

Vijnanamaya kosha
The sheath of intellect.
Vyadhi
Physical ailment.

Y

Yama
Ethical codes for daily life: how we conduct ourselves towards others.
Yoga Sutras
The aphorisms on the practice of yoga attributed to Sage Patanjali.

No term matches that. Try a shorter word.

Meanings follow the glossary of the Ramamani Iyengar Memorial Yoga Institute, Pune ( bksiyengar.com ), shortened and reworded.
